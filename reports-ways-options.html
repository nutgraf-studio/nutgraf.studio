Outwards and Inwards, one animation

The live-reports section of /reports/ has two cards with a diagram each. Here the two diagrams become one that says both: the same live report speaks to the people outside the client (funders, partners, press, public) and to the decision makers inside it. Each take is the real mint section with the real copy. ?solo=1 shows one take, ?at=3 freezes every take at 3 s.

On /reports/ until 13 November 2026: one diagram per card. Outwards the arrows leave the report through a ring to the people outside; inwards a wall holds everyone and the arrows converge on the report.
