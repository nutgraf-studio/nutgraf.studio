Our flagship service

A flagship report carries a year of research, thousands of numbers and an organisation’s name. We make every one of those numbers count: checked against its source, given the form its finding needs, set on a page people want to read.

  • Built for heavy data

    Dozens of charts, one visual system. We design the system first, so the thirty-eighth figure reads as easily as the first.

  • Bespoke, chart by chart

    No templates. Flows become a Sankey, 38 countries become a heatmap, and sometimes the right chart is a single number.

  • Checked before it’s drawn

    Every figure is traced back to its dataset before it reaches the page, and checked again on the proof.

How it works

From raw data to final proof.

  1. Data audit

    We start with the data, not the layout: what’s there, what’s missing, and what the numbers can honestly carry.

  2. Chart system

    Palette, type and chart grammar for the whole report, so every figure speaks the same language.

  3. Design and verify

    Every page laid out, every number checked against its source, on screen and again on the proof.

  4. Publish, then extend

    Print-ready PDF and digital edition, social cards for launch day, and a dashboard when you want the data to keep working.
