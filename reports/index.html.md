## Case study 1: Beyond borders (Afrobarometer, African insights 2026)

*Beyond borders: Citizen perspectives on a shifting global order* is Afrobarometer's flagship report for 2026. It asks how Africans see a world in flux: free trade under the AfCFTA, migration, the great powers, the continent's voice in global institutions and climate action.

- Client: Afrobarometer, pan-African research network
- Pages: 48, in English
- Figures: 38, plus 4 tables, in a single chart system, from a 38-country heatmap to one-page country scorecards
- Data: Afrobarometer Round 10 survey, 38 countries, 2024/2025; face-to-face interviews, nationally representative samples
- Nutgraf's role: report design (editorial design, data visualisation and infographics). The report's colophon (p. 2) reads "Report design by nutgraf.studio".
- The report: https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/african-insights-2026-beyond-borders-citizen-perspectives-on-a-shifting-global-order/

## Case study 2: Rețele Electrice România, Sustainability Report 2024 (PPC Group)

*Raport de Sustenabilitate 2024* is the first sustainability report Rețele Electrice România prepared with reference to ESRS, the EU's sustainability reporting standards. Rețele Electrice is PPC Group's electricity distributor in Romania, covering Banat, Dobrogea and Muntenia. The report covers materiality, governance, climate, people and the modernisation of the grid.

- Client: Rețele Electrice România, PPC Group
- Pages: 105, in Romanian
- Framework: ESRS, the reporting standards of the EU's CSRD
- Nutgraf's role: editorial design, data visualisation and KPI infographics: KPI tiles, flows and rings
- Featured page: page 49, a Sankey diagram tracing 793,348 tCO₂e of gross emissions from three scopes to eleven sources

## From PDF to interactive dashboard

A PDF is a snapshot. Nutgraf connects a report's charts to the datasets behind them and turns them into interactive dashboards, so the figures can be filtered, refreshed and explored.

- **Outwards: show your impact.** Data-driven storytelling for funders, partners, the press and the public: shareable charts, country and programme pages, and a number behind every claim.
- **Inwards: decide with data.** The same datasets for boards and teams: indicators that refresh as new data arrives, filters by region, year or programme, and the view each decision needs.

Three live demos on the page, each rebuilt from a printed figure:

1. **Democracy (a streamgraph).** Thirty countries over five Afrobarometer survey rounds, 2011/2013 to 2021/2023: the share who prefer democracy and the share satisfied with how it works. Source: *African insights 2024*, Figure A.1, page 41.
2. **Leaving home (a dot plot).** The share who have considered emigrating "a lot", 2016/2018 against 2024/2025, in 31 countries, with a region filter and sorting. Source: *Beyond borders*, Figure 10, page 15.
3. **Major powers (one table, two dashboards).** The share who see the influence of China, the United States, the European Union, the former colonial power, India and Russia as positive, in 38 countries. The outwards view writes the finding as a sentence; the inwards view adds each country's rank and gap from the average. Source: *Beyond borders*, Table 2, page 28.

## How we design a data report

1. **Data audit.** We start with the data, not the layout: what's there, what's missing, and what the numbers can honestly carry.
2. **Chart system.** Palette, type and chart grammar for the whole report, so every figure speaks the same language.
3. **Design and verify.** Every page laid out, every number checked against its source, on screen and again on the proof.
4. **Publish, then extend.** Print-ready PDF and digital edition, social cards for launch day, and a dashboard when you want the data to keep working.

Made for: flagship reports, sustainability and ESG reports, annual reports, survey research, policy briefs, impact reports, infographics and interactive dashboards.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does a data report design studio do?**
We turn research, survey and ESG data into reports people read. We audit the data, build one chart system for the whole report, design every page, chart and infographic, and check every number against its source before it goes to print. Recent work: Afrobarometer's 2026 flagship report and Rețele Electrice România's 2024 sustainability report.

**Can you turn a PDF report into an interactive dashboard?**
Yes. We connect a report's charts to the datasets behind them, so the figures can be filtered, ranked and refreshed. On the page, page 49 of the Rețele Electrice report is rebuilt as an interactive Sankey diagram, and two figures and a table from Afrobarometer reports become live dashboards.

**Do you design infographics and data visualisations (dataviz) for reports?**
Yes: charts, tables, KPI tiles, Sankey diagrams, heatmaps, dot plots and one-page country scorecards. Each is drawn for its finding and built to one visual system, so a report with 38 figures, like *Beyond borders*, still reads as one piece.

**Do you design sustainability and ESG reports?**
Yes. We designed Rețele Electrice România's 2024 sustainability report, the company's first prepared with reference to ESRS, the EU's sustainability reporting standards: 105 pages of emissions, energy, workforce and EU Taxonomy data, turned into KPI tiles, flows and rings a non-specialist can read.

**What is data-driven storytelling?**
Leading with the finding and letting the data carry it: a clear headline, the chart that proves it and the context that explains it. Inside a report it sets the order of the pages; outside it, it turns the same figures into stories for funders, partners, the press and the public.
