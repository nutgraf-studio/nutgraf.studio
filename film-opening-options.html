Exhibitions: how the film opens

The intro irises out and zooms back from the hero film. That zoom used to land on the man in the navy suit. Each take below is a re-edit of the same Între noi loop: it opens on a different shot and drops his shot from the loop. The film rewinds to the start when the ring opens, so the zoom always lands on the opening shot. B has been live on the Exhibitions page since 23 October 2026.

AThe charts

The dot-chart panels at eye level, with visitors' legs beneath them. One 4-second shot holds through the whole zoom, so the charts come first and no faces appear.

BVisitors reading

Three visitors, seen from behind, reading the chord diagram. One 4-second shot shows people engaging with the data without making any one of them the subject.

CThe title, then a chart

The porthole opens on the Între noi banner, then cuts to a chart close-up and the wide hall as the zoom finishes. The show's name comes first, with two quick cuts during the zoom.
