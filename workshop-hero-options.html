Workshops — hero backgrounds

Option 2, Sort, shipped on 23 October 2026. Four simple animations behind the hero of /workshops/: each frame is the real page, which now carries Sort; the other frames hide it and slip their own canvas in behind the headline. All four stay faint so the white headline and black copy keep their contrast, pause when the hero scrolls away, and show one still frame to visitors who ask for reduced motion. Click a frame to open it full size below.

1Ripple

The dot grid of the home page's Workshops card, with two slow rings pulsing out from behind the icosahedron. The dots swell and turn mint as a ring passes.

2Sort (live)

Loose data points drift across the violet, then file into a chart along the foot of the hero, column by column, light up mint, and let go again. A 15-second loop.

3Plot

Faint graph paper, and three lines that plot themselves from left to right, one in mint, each with a pen-tip dot, then fade and start a new trace.

4Contours

Contour lines of a slowly shifting surface, like a map of the data, with one level in mint. The calmest of the four: the lines never stop, but barely move.
