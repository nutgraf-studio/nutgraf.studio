Highlighting Workshops & Exhibitions

Six ways to set the two service pages apart from the in-page links. Each option is shown on all four bars it has to live on — hover the links to see the hover state. The highlighted links carry a page-link class, so whichever you pick is a class plus a few lines of CSS on each page.

1Arrow out

A small accent arrow after the label says “this is its own page”. On hover the arrow nudges up and out, and the usual underline appears. The quietest option.

2Outline pill

A ghost version of the contact chip: same shape, hairline outline, no fill — so it reads as a destination while the filled chip stays the one action. Hover firms up the outline and adds a faint tint.

3Marker stroke

A thick, slightly tilted highlighter stroke under the label, mint on dark bars and violet on cream. On hover the stroke fills the whole word, the way the chip’s wipe fills the pill.

4Serif italic

The two labels switch to Fraunces italic, the same voice as the italic words in the headlines. All typography, no new shapes. Hover draws the underline in the accent colour.

5Ghost offset

The layered-wordmark treatment from the brand: bold label with an offset ghost copy behind it in mint or violet. On hover the ghost slides further out.

6Phosphor dot

A small dot before each label that slowly glows and dims, like a pixel on the hero film’s phosphor screen. Violet on cream, mint elsewhere. Stays still for people who turn off motion.
