Menu bar — logo animation options

Each take plays once when the page loads and again on hover (or keyboard focus) over the logo. Hit Replay to see the entrance again. The three bars are real size: over the hero film, the cream bar after scrolling on Home, and the violet bar on Workshops. The last row is the same logo at 2.25×.

AAssemble

The eight shards fly in from around the tile, each with a small twist, and click into the sphere one after another. The letters of “nutgraf” rise in right behind. On hover the shards push out a few pixels and snap back.

BCrack

Logo at rest on load, then a single quick crack after a beat — the shards part along the seams and close again. On hover the nut stays open while the pointer is on it and shuts when it leaves.

CSpin

The sphere spins in from three-quarters of a turn back and settles with a small overshoot; the letters slide in from the left. On hover it makes one full turn inside the tile.

DScan

A flash runs around the shards in a circle, like the hero’s loader: violet on the cream bar, mint over the film and on violet. Nothing moves, only colour. Hover runs the sweep again.

ETypewriter

The mark pops in, then “nutgraf” types itself out letter by letter behind an accent caret, which blinks twice at the end and goes. On hover the letters ripple up and down in a wave.

FBreathe — continuous

No entrance: the shards drift apart by a pixel or two and back on a slow five-second loop, each a little out of step, so the nut always looks alive. Hover holds it still. The only take that never stops — the quietest, but also the one most likely to distract.

All takes go static with Reduce motion turned on. Once you pick one it gets ported to the menu bar of Home, Workshops and Exhibitions, with the logo inlined instead of the current <img>.
